FAQ

Questions we hear before most projects.

Straight answers on scope, pricing, ownership and support. Can't find yours? Ask us directly.

support@iphoenixthemes.com
Can you customise a theme I bought on ThemeForest?

Yes — whether it is one of ours or another author's. We can restyle it, add sections and features, or fix whatever is slowing it down. Send us your store URL and a list of what you'd like to change.

Do you work with clients outside Vietnam?

Yes. Our themes are used by merchants in many countries, and we run projects remotely in English — with written specs, regular progress previews and updates that work across time zones.

How is the price decided?

After a short discovery conversation we fix the scope and send a quote with a clear price and timeline. No open-ended hourly billing — anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. Small theme changes can take days; a full store, website or web app is planned in phases. Firm dates are written into the quote, and you see working progress along the way.

Who owns the code when the project is done?

You do. We hand over the full source code, accounts and documentation, so you are never locked in to us.

Can you take over a site someone else built?

Usually, yes. We start with a code review, tell you honestly what is worth keeping, then fix, upgrade or rebuild — whichever costs you less in the long run.

Do you use AI to write the code?

We use AI tools for routine work, which shortens turnaround — but a developer owns every decision and reviews every line before it ships. You get the speed without giving up quality or accountability.

Do you support the site after launch?

Yes. We stay available for fixes, small changes and advice after launch, and can take on ongoing maintenance if you need it.
